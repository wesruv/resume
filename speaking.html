CSS Trainings

This presentation is a labor of love. It's intended to be a full day training, but I've given it in part or whole at events or for my coworkers.

The focus is teaching the "why" behind CSS: document flow, box model, animation, and the behavior behind different layout systems; in hopes others will understand and enjoy CSS as much as I do.
